# Agent Bot Catalogs (Curated Lists of Ready-Made Bots)

A recurring bookmark pattern: someone publishes a numbered list of ready-made agent bots or repos and says to start from one instead of building from scratch. Three sources so far, all about [[wiki/entities/tools/grok-bot|Grok Bot]]; the lists are promotional-leaning and most claims are the authors' own.

## Lists

- [[raw/2026-11-08-irongiantXBT-2119484092089762141|@irongiantXBT]], "10 Bots That Work While You Sleep": personal X assistant, competitor monitor, lead list builder, group chat manager, subscription auditor, content repurposer, morning brief, Grok Swarm (orchestrator with up to 10 specialist bots), build-anything agent, and a second brain. Each has a schedule, what it replaces and a "never" line (e.g. never posts, never sends, never cancels). Stats cited in the article (Gartner, Microsoft, C+R, McKinsey) are the author's and not checked here.
- [[raw/2026-11-08-monokern-2119446181231067489|@monokern]]: first 6 bots he would add: Reverse Engineer, Research Desk, Proto Bot (credited to Shub Gaur at SpaceX Super Intelligence), tinkabot (credited to Lauren Tan at SpaceXSI), Clip Bot, Outbound Desk. He claims the setup gives him back about 4 hours a day. Bot links point at x.ai/bot/... pages that were not fetched.
- [[raw/2026-11-06-s4yonnara-2118814330695073907|@s4yonnara]]: 15 GitHub repos for agent teams and trading.

## Common thread (synthesis)

Every list gives each bot one job and an explicit approval boundary (the "never" lines), echoing the "where it must stop" advice in @s4yonnara's guide. The second-brain bot (#1 in @irongiantXBT's list) mirrors this vault's own pattern: see [[wiki/concepts/llm-knowledge-bases|LLM knowledge bases]].
