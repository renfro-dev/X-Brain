# AI-Native Services (Selling the Work, not the Software)

The thesis that AI value goes to companies selling outcomes (services), not seats of software.

## Claims
- [[raw/2026-10-30-guilleflorvs-2116109740832936033|@guilleflorvs]] relays Sequoia's thesis that "the next $1T company will sell work, not software". Copilots compete with every model release; selling the outcome ("books closed, contracts reviewed, claims handled") means each model improvement raises margins. He says "for every $1 spent on software, ~$6 is spent on services" (his figure, uncited in the capture). "Not 'AI for accountants.' The AI accounting firm."
- [[raw/2026-10-30-gregisenberg-2116212861466755509|@gregisenberg]]: "the future of SaaS in 1 image": 1) give away SaaS free or nearly, 2) upsell AI agents, 3) upsell agency services. Quotes his own "SaaS is dead" video post.

## Synthesis
Both put the money in services wrapped around software. Flor describes a services firm built on software; Isenberg describes software used as a free funnel into agents and services. Related: [[wiki/concepts/agentic-org-maturity|Agentic org maturity]].
