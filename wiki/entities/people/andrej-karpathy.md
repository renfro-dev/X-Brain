# Andrej Karpathy

Not a bookmarked author here; credited by both second-brain sources as the originator of the LLM Wiki pattern — "a gist he published in April 2026" ([[raw/2026-09-28-rvaniaaaa-2104602707675718102|@rvaniaaaa]]); "described the pattern in April 2026… published it as an idea file, on purpose" ([[raw/2026-10-25-danikkk_wqs-2114344135415021632|@danikkk_wqs]]). [[raw/2026-09-28-rvaniaaaa-2104602707675718102|@rvaniaaaa]] quotes him: "The act of collecting information is effortless. The act of keeping fifty interlinked notes current, consistent, and cross-referenced is the work that no human sustains."

Concept: [[wiki/concepts/llm-knowledge-bases|LLM knowledge bases]].
